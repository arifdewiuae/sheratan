BRAND IDENTITY — β Arietis
sheratan

Three points of unequal magnitude — γ, β, α — rising along one shallow curve. β is the brightest and sits at the centre: the reference point, the boundary marker. The line that carries them is a hairline, drawn with an instrument, never a gesture.

01 — Logomark

Drawn on a 64-unit square. Points at (8, 48), (30.5, 37.2) and (56, 16), radii 1.4 / 3.2 / 2 — a fixed 1 : 2.3 : 1.4 magnitude ratio. The curve is a single quadratic, control point (33, 38).

Clear space equals the radius of β multiplied by four on all sides. Nothing enters the square; the mark is never cropped by it.

Never rotated, mirrored, re-spaced, outlined, boxed, filled with a tint, or given rays. No variant carries colour.

Surviving 16px

At 16 pixels the curve is dropped and the three points are redrawn on the grid: γ a single pixel, β a five-pixel diamond, α a 2×2 block, holding the same rise and the same spacing. Magnitude survives as pixel count, which is what the mark was always about — the ranking of the three points, not the line between them.

05 — Dark inversion
sheratan

On black the curve lifts from 12% to 30% — light strokes read thinner than dark ones of equal width. The three points stay pure #FAFAF8 at full opacity.
